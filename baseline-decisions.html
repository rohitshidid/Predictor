CPL 2026 preseason power rankings

Nobody has bowled a ball yet, so this is a projection, not a table. Trinbago start on top because champions usually do — but the gap behind them is thinner than last September made it look.

Champhunt · Willow SportsBefore round one

Seven teams this year, up from six. Jamaica are back after seven years away, Barbados are the Tridents again, and everyone plays ten league games instead of eight. That is the shape of it. What follows is where each side starts and why.

One thing worth saying before the list: a preseason ranking is not last season's table with the names shuffled. Saint Lucia finished top in 2025 and start fifth here, because they lost their captain, their leading spinner and Tim David in one off-season. Trinbago finished third and start first, because they won the thing. The order below is what the numbers say once you account for who actually left.

Champions, and they had to do it the hard way — third in the league on net run rate, then three straight in the playoffs. Andre Russell and Keacy Carty have gone to Jamaica, which is a real cost. But this is the same core that has won five titles, and nobody else starts with that.
