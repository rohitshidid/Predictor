4. What changed between 2025 and 2026

Each team now plays ten league games instead of eight. St Vincent & the Grenadines hosts CPL matches for the first time, staging the opening three-match phase at Arnos Vale.

Franchises

Two changes, both material:

  • Jamaica Kingsmen — new team. Jamaica returns after a seven-year absence. Confirmed February 2026, named March 2026. Rovman Powell captains, Grant Bradburn coaches. In the draft the Kingsmen held the first three picks, restricted to Jamaican players, and could take only one player from an existing squad.
  • Barbados Royals are now the Barbados Tridents. Announced May 2026 under a "One Barbados" initiative — the Tridents name returns, the squad carries over largely intact. ~~This contradicts data/cpl_2026.json, which still lists "Barbados Royals".~~ Resolved 2 August 2026: the data file now carries Barbados Tridents (short BT), and the franchise inherits the Royals' 2025 record as its starting rating via priorFrom. Only two 2026 captaincies are public: Moeen Ali at Antigua and Rovman Powell at Jamaica. Powell captained Barbados in 2025, so both confirmed captaincies are changes. Six of the seven coaching appointments for 2026 are unannounced as of 1 August 2026.

Draft rules, 2026

Squads of 17: nine West Indian, five overseas, three breakout players. The six returning teams each got four Right to Match options (three on any salary slot, one restricted to slots 7–17 and to a home-territory player). Jamaica got the first three picks, Jamaican players only, and could take just one player from an existing squad; any team losing a player to Jamaica received an extra RMO. Maximum four overseas players per match, minimum one breakout player.

5. Caveats

  • Wikipedia's 2025 squad tables list the players signed pre-season. Several who appear in scorecards are not in them — Andries Gous, Rassie van der Dussen, Chris Green, Salman Irshad, Saurabh Netravalkar, Iftikhar Ahmed — so they were in-season signings or replacements. The movement counts in section 4 are built from the squad tables and will slightly understate churn.
  • The 2026 squads were current as of the 25 July 2026 ESPNcricinfo report cited on the season page. Signings after that date are not reflected.
  • 2026 coaching staff and captaincy are mostly unannounced; blank cells mean "not published", not "unchanged".
